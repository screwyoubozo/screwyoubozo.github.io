Soapbox: My rules and etiquette standards

This title is a bit misleading because... well, rules are made to be broken. At least, some of them ought to be.

Why does this exist?

  • The first step to solving a problem is to identify a problem. More often than not, any experiences of panic, anguish, frustration, or similar that I experience can be attributable to a rule that I impose on myself (or perhaps on others).
  • Having a set of rules allows me to interact with others in a palatable manner.
  • In light of this, some of these rules contradict one another. I wonder if I'll be able to catch discrepancies between my conscious and subconscious?

Potential consequences if I fail to follow my rules

  • Irreversible damage for interpersonal relationships, including those that have nothing to do with me
  • Decreased likelihood of fitting in, belonging, etc.
  • Self-loathing; festering of concerns

Rules imposed on myself

Who knows if any of these rules end up bleeding over to the next category?

Interpersonal interactions

Core rule: Others around me are to be treated with the utmost respect; however, no expectation of reciprocity should be made.

  • I recognise that I am different from others; as such, I expect myself to accommodate to others. I will not expect others to accommodate to me.
  • I will not unnecessarily burden others as a result of my decisions.
  • I will not impose too much on others.
  • Even if an event, hangout, gathering, etc. is mentioned, even by someone familiar, I will not invite myself or ask for an invite. The other party must explicitly invite me. Rationale: others' interactions with others are not mine to infringe upon.
  • The world does not revolve around me. I will not talk about myself unless someone else has talked about themselves, and even when talking about myself, I will limit the amount of output such as not to shift any significant importance towards me.
  • I will not burden others with burdens that are only meant to be mine. This includes rules that only apply to myself.
  • I am expected to exercise basic courtesy. For any potential or perceived infringement, I will exercise manners and apologise should I have not been wronged.

Rules imposed on everyone (and yes, that includes myself)

  • Everyone must try their best and put forth their best honest effort.
    • Cheating (such as in an academic context) or playing dirty (or dishonestly) as a means to gain an unfair advantage will not be tolerated.
    • Accepting that your best effort might not be "enough" goes a long way. If your best isn't enough, all that is left to do is to keep trying.
    • My best performance is not the same as someone else's best performance. It is unwise to declare that you did not do your best, just because someone surpassed you.
    • Giving up may sometimes be the best course of action; however, if an alternative option is better, then do not give up.
  • Only seek help for the sake of seeking help. Do not "seek help" as a pretext for "seeking validation."
    • Corollary: Accept that advice may hurt, just like how children have to learn to eat their vegetables.
