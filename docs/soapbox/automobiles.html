Driving with a bunch of other angry white drivers

The title is a bit misleading because this title represents a stereotype, especially in the United States.

Some may call this "racism." I won't provide my verdict on that claim (I have bigger fish to fry), but I prefer the phrase "empirical observation" as that represents my conclusion more accurately.

And just remember. I can't observe what is not in my environment :)

As I drive some of my family members to a bingsu store around 19:00, I have my car's lights set to "automatic." They were illuminating as a result. What I did not realise, however, was that my brights (it was something blue, was it highbeams?) were also turned on.

Once I drove into the plaza, I drop off my passengers and proceed to find a parking spot. As soon as I leave, some white guy driving in a big-ass red pickup truck confronts me, from within his truck:

"You stupid fucking idiot, your brights were on. Why the fuck do you have your fucking bright lights on?" I think that's what he said? But this guy was cussing every other word, so hell if I know what he even said.

What would you do in this situation?

I could very easily tell that this guy was trying to intimidate me or similar; he was not there for a polite or conciliatory discussion. I have had moments where I forgot to turn on my headlights, and another driver in the next lane (while at a red light) shouted at us very succinctly: "Turn on your lights!" We responded with "Thank you!" - the message was straight to the point.

Unfortunately, people in the United States (especially white people) are very easy to piss off, and it doesn't take that much. Teehee.

So I could either try to apologise or get the guy to buzz off. Even though I may have faulted him for having my brights on (unknowingly), I could tell that he is not going to accept an apology without talking his ass off more.

Thus, I have two words to offer:

"No English." (spoken in a Vietnamese accent)

The guy ended up swearing at me more, but he drove off ten seconds later because whatever he said I wasn't going to understand. Or at the very least, I have convinced him as such.

Oh, and by the way. I memorised and observed the car from a few intersections, and that bucko went into the plaza just to start an unnecessary argument with me. Stay safe in the US; people can be unreasonably petty. I have bigger fish to fry.

I know that the title of this soapbox includes "drivers" but I only talk about one. Well, on a drive to California, my mom's family friend was confronted by a different white guy for god-knows-why. But those two magic words got him to buzz off.
